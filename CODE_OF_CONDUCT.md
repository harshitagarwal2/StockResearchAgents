# Community code of conduct

StockResearchAgents is committed to a professional, welcoming, and technically rigorous community.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Be specific, constructive, and willing to correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy, licensing, entitlement, and security boundaries.
- Keep financial-research claims proportional to the available evidence.
- Help newcomers reproduce results and understand project terminology.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, sexualized attention, publication of private information, credential exposure, and knowingly deceptive research or performance claims are not accepted in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-affiliated communication. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not include credentials, private financial information, or unrelated personal data. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, recurrence, remediation, and the safety of affected participants. Good-faith technical disagreement is not misconduct; personal abuse and intimidation are.
